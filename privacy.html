Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Summary

protonhackers Publisher runs entirely on the creator's own computer. It has no server, no database, no analytics and no advertising. It does not sell or share any data.

Data the tool handles

  • TikTok access and refresh tokens: received after you approve access on TikTok's page. They are stored only on your computer, in a file readable only by your user account, and are used only to call TikTok's API on your behalf.
  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic: open ID, display name, avatar): shown in the tool so you can see which account is connected. It is not stored elsewhere.
  • Your videos and captions: read from your computer and sent directly to TikTok when you upload.
  • A local upload log: date, file name and TikTok publish ID, kept on your computer.

Sharing

Data is sent only to TikTok, through its official API, to perform the upload you request. Nothing is sent to the operator or to any other third party.

Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on your computer until you delete the token file or revoke access in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services), after which they stop working. Deleting the tool's folder removes the upload log.

Children

The tool is not intended for people under 18.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the effective date shows the latest version. Questions: @protonhackers on Instagram.
